# Pricing research and commercial recommendation

Date checked: 2026-09-15. Audience: internal planning for smolikja team.
This is a recommendation, not a published price list or a binding offer.
The related [landing page plan](landing-page-redesign-plan.md) incorporates
the customer-facing conclusions.

## 1. Recommended decision

Use a free introductory consultation, paid research/design when the brief
requires it, and an explicitly scoped delivery proposal. Offer a fixed price
for sufficiently understood work, or staged development with an agreed budget
ceiling when priorities are still evolving. Agree maintenance and ongoing
development separately.

Launch the landing page with **"Úvodní konzultace zdarma"** and an explanation
of how scope and price are agreed. Do not initially publish a universal
**"MVP za 50 000 Kč"** package. A narrowly defined product at that price can
still be a valid individual offer when its economics work.

The owner's reference project — three screens for CZK 50,000, estimated at
one week and taking two elapsed weeks — suggests checking estimation and
scope control. It does not establish a market price ceiling or, without actual
hours and costs, whether that job was profitable.

### Interpretation for Smolikja

- A price above CZK 50,000 is compatible with the observed public offers.
  The sample cannot tell us how many customers will buy at any particular price.
- "MVP" is not a standard package. A prototype, a narrow production app,
  and an app with administration and multiple integrations are different offers.
- Screen count is a poor primary estimator. Accounts, permissions, data,
  external systems, device behaviour, error cases and release work can dominate
  a three-screen application.
- The appropriate price must cover Smolikja's actual delivery effort and
  commercial overhead. A competitor's "from" price is useful positioning
  context, not a budget calculation.
- Validate willingness to pay through scoped proposals and the reasons behind
  won/lost opportunities. Record scope alongside the price so comparisons mean
  something. Do not interpret a small sample as price elasticity.

## 3. The historical CZK 50,000 project

Two elapsed weeks are not necessarily 80 working hours. The following scenarios
assume CZK 50,000 is revenue available to the business before operating costs;
if that total includes VAT or pass-through expenses, use the corresponding net
service revenue instead. These are calculations, not an estimate of take-home pay.

| Actual hours allocated to that engagement | Revenue per hour at CZK 50,000 |
| --- | --- |
| 40 | CZK 1,250 |
| 80 | CZK 625 |
| 100 | CZK 500 |

Include briefing, implementation, communication, testing, release, handover and
any agreed post-delivery fixes in the time record. Allocate acquisition costs
from unsuccessful leads and general business overhead separately, without
double-counting them in both hours and the costing rate.

If the job really consumed about 80 hours, CZK 625/hour leaves little room for
the proposed team service once collaborators, tools and non-billable work are
funded. That is an economic concern to check, not a claim that the project made
a loss. Assess contribution after direct costs and whether the remaining revenue
supports the studio's overhead and income target.

## 4. Free introduction and paid work

The owner's desired distinction is sound: learning about the client's situation
can be a free sales activity; producing a researched solution is paid delivery.
Make the boundary visible before work starts.

| Step | Recommended practical scope | Payment and output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory consultation | Usually 60–90 minutes; understand the business, intended users, existing systems, budget and main unknowns | Free. A brief recap, indication of fit and proposed next step; not a competitor report or full product specification |
| Longer introductory visit | Individually agreed when observing the client's environment is valuable | Can be free at Smolikja's discretion. Treat preparation, travel and follow-up as an acquisition investment, not a universal website entitlement |
| Definition of the first version | Organise available requirements, identify risks, outline the main flow and prioritise scope | Paid, bounded deliverable and price agreed first. The client receives a usable brief and proposed next step |
| Research and design | As needed: competitive review, customer interviews/testing, product hypotheses and prototype | Paid scope tailored to the uncertainty. Define what will be investigated and delivered; comprehensive research does not fit every small budget |

A simple brief with a reliable estimate can proceed directly from the free
consultation to a fixed-price development offer. Do not require a discovery
package merely to unlock a price quote. Where important uncertainties prevent
pricing, explain precisely what paid work will resolve them.

### Why a free day should be an individual decision

Illustrative acquisition calculation: at a hypothetical 25% lead-to-contract
rate, spending two hours per prospect allocates eight hours to each won
engagement. Spending eight hours per prospect allocates 32 hours per win,
before travel. The conversion assumption is not observed Smolikja data.

A full day may be worthwhile for a strong prospective long-term relationship.
For a CZK 50,000 project it is a substantial commitment. Define a normal free
allowance internally, track it and allow deliberate exceptions. Do not promise
"as much time as needed" on the landing page.

## 5. Working pricing model

These ranges are recommendations to validate against costs and delivery data.
They are not inferred market averages, approved prices or website copy. All
calculations use service revenue before any applicable VAT and pass-through
charges; a final offer must state its actual price basis.

### Definition and design

- **CZK 10,000–25,000:** a small, bounded definition stage based mainly on
  information the client already has, with a written scope and main flow.
  This does not include extensive user recruitment or full competitive research.
- **CZK 25,000–60,000:** a more involved research/design engagement with selected
  research questions and a prototype or test plan. Research depth and the number
  of interviews/iterations must fit the estimate. Recruitment and external costs
  are explicitly included or separate.
- Larger or unusually uncertain discovery is quoted individually. Package the
  output and scope, not an unrestricted number of meetings or all possible
  research methods.

### Development

- **CZK 80,000–150,000:** a working proposal band for a tightly scoped first
  usable app with one core journey and few unknowns, particularly where the
  design, backend or reusable foundation already exists.
- **CZK 150,000–300,000+:** a starting planning band when the delivery includes
  more substantial backend work, administration, integrations or a broader
  product/release scope. These features are not all automatically included at
  the low end.
- **Around CZK 50,000:** reserve for an individual, demonstrably small delivery
  or a prototype with matching scope. A production MVP at this price is possible
  only if the estimated work, quality checks and risk allowance fit the budget.
  Do not silently substitute a demo for the production result the client expects.

At an illustrative internal selling rate of CZK 1,500/hour and a 25% effort
contingency, CZK 50,000 buys approximately 26.7 estimated delivery hours. All
agreed delivery tasks must fit those hours, not only coding. The rate is a
costing assumption to validate, not an industry norm.

### How to calculate a fixed quote

Use an internal selling rate that already supports expected non-billable time,
business overhead and the desired margin. Price the agreed delivery effort,
add an explicit allowance for identifiable uncertainty, and include external
costs on a clear basis. Do not blindly add multiple margins to the same costs.

Illustration for a project estimated at 80 total delivery hours:

```text
80 hours × CZK 1,500/hour = CZK 120,000
25% effort contingency   = CZK  30,000
Example fixed quote      = CZK 150,000
```

The 25% is a planning allowance, not a measured risk rate or a 25% profit
margin. If the work still has a realistic chance of doubling in effort, resolve
the uncertainty or use a staged model; a small blanket buffer is insufficient.
The 80 hours must include design, communication, verification and release where
those are part of the offer. Check the result against the client's expected
value and alternatives; internal arithmetic does not establish willingness to pay.

## 6. Fixed price, budget ceiling and monthly capacity

| Model | Appropriate use | What is actually fixed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed-price delivery | Clear scope, known dependencies and testable acceptance conditions | Price and agreed deliverable; changes outside scope are estimated and agreed separately |
| Development with a budget ceiling | Priorities or the solution may change as the team learns | Maximum authorised spending for the stage; scope is prioritised within it, not every wishlist item guaranteed |
| Recurring capacity retainer | Ongoing improvement and support after a working relationship exists | Agreed capacity/service coverage per period; it is not unlimited feature development |

For a fixed-price MVP, write down the core journey, platforms, supported roles,
integrations, delivery/distribution method, testing, feedback rounds, acceptance
conditions and handover. Clarify how defects differ from changed requirements.
List external accounts, licences, hosting and future maintenance as included
or separate. Use milestone billing appropriate to the engagement.

For a budget ceiling, show progress and spend regularly. Before the ceiling is
reached, agree to reduce/reorder scope, pause, or authorise another stage. Do not
describe this model as a fixed-price promise to complete an undefined product.

A monthly retainer can support the desired long relationship, but its capacity,
response expectations, rollover rules and included costs must be defined. Avoid
launching a cheap subscription that implicitly finances an unrestricted build.

## 7. AI-assisted development and an MVP package

AI assistance is part of delivery practice. Use demonstrated time savings in
estimation, while retaining responsibility for design, integration, review,
testing, security, release and maintainability. Do not assume a constant speedup
or price based only on how quickly screens can be generated.

"Vibe-coded MVP" is not recommended as the main buyer-facing offer. The customer
needs a clear outcome, limits and a dependable team. A suitable package name
could be **"První verze aplikace"** or **"Ověření jednoho klíčového scénáře"**.

Define whether the output is a clickable prototype, a technical experiment,
a pilot for a test group, or a production app. These are all useful, distinct
deliverables. A fixed package is viable when repeated delivery establishes its
scope, typical effort and risk. Review several comparable engagements before
advertising a universal price or turnaround; that review is a practical
calibration exercise, not statistical validation.

> Po úvodní konzultaci navrhneme další postup. Pro jasně vymezené zadání
> nabídneme pevnou cenu; u postupného vývoje si domluvíme rozpočet a priority.

### Free versus paid FAQ

> Úvodní konzultace je zdarma. Podrobnější průzkum, analýzu a návrh řešení
> naceníme podle dohodnutého rozsahu. S placenou prací začneme až po vašem
> odsouhlasení.

Keep internal hourly calculations and speculative package prices out of the
landing page. After calibration, a genuinely deliverable "from" price with
concrete scope or example projects with agreed budgets can help qualify leads.
Publish it only when the team can support it and disclosure is appropriate.
